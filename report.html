Benchmark: all 6,300 runs

Gemini repeats far more than the GPT-5.6 models, but the rates are small overall.

Difficulty

Harder tasks take more tool calls and are solved less often, yet the exact-repeat rate stays flat — the draft's 'repetition grows with difficulty' claim is not supported.

Pilot: repetition vs. redundancy

LLM-judged redundant work is 0–2.4% of operations.

Repeats vs. structural labels

Most exact repeats were useful (e.g. test reruns after an edit); most redundant ops were not exact repeats (e.g. same content read with a different command).
